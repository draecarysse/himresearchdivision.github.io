I. Background — The Five Descents

Since the dawn of creation, life on Earth has stood on the brink of extinction five times. Official records attribute each mass extinction to natural disasters, climate shifts, volcanic eruptions, or meteor impacts. What the official records OMITTED is that each extinction was preceded by the appearance of a being that did not belong to the world of men.

These beings arrived when life was on the brink of the abyss and disappeared once the danger had passed. The first was recorded solely as a BLOOD-FEEDER OF IMMENSE POWER, capable of destroying all life on the planet with a single breath. The second was associated with FIRE, BLOOD, AND AN UNCONTAINABLE FORCE. The third's existence became linked to the FORESTS AND THE MEMORY OF NATURE. The fourth's true nature remained HIDDEN EVEN FROM THOSE WHO ENCOUNTERED IT.

After them, the fifth would come.

II. The Fifth Extinction

The fifth extinction was unlike any of the previous ones. This time, the threat did not originate from the Earth. It emerged from a place that documents referred to only as "the Abyss". Some records claimed it was a fissure beneath the Earth's surface; others spoke of a gateway that had appeared in the heavens.

From that opening emerged creatures of impossible forms that appeared at night and vanished before dawn. They took on appearances that humans could recognize, and among them all, one recurred with particular frequency: THAT OF ANGELS. They possessed wings, emitted a light similar to that of the stars, and descended upon the cities with voices promising salvation.

Humanity believed that, at last, the help it had awaited for centuries had arrived. It was mistaken. Those creatures had not come to save them. They were IMPOSTORS who had learned to take on the appearance of that which humans considered sacred in order to deceive them. Cities began to fall, one after another. The skies darkened, and daylight could no longer reach the Earth's surface. Neither weapons, nor armies, nor governments were able to hold back the creatures.

Humanity was on the verge of facing something worse than its own extinction.

III. The Descent of Subject 1510

And then the miracle happened. In the midst of that darkness, a golden light pierced the skies shrouded in a black mist. A figure was plummeting through the clouds, enveloped in a radiance so intense that, for a few moments, night seemed to turn into day.

"It was an angel. A true one. He descended upon the Earth and fought the impostors. One by one, those beings were eradicated until the last one fell and the world was once again silent."

— Field testimony, recovered from Archive HIM-0005

Earth had been saved, but the price proved too high. The light enveloping the angel began to fade; blood flowed from his body, and, consumed by exhaustion, he finally COLLAPSED ONTO THE ASHES. A scientific team managed to locate him before he disappeared and transported him to a SECRET FACILITY, far from the city.

IV. Anatomical Study — Subject 1510

For months the research team studied his body. The following findings were recorded in the primary investigation log:

They gave him numerous names and classifications throughout the investigation, but only one prevailed: Subject 1510. No one was able to discover his true name. Nor his origin. And, despite all the experiments conducted, none of the researchers could explain how such a creature had made its way to Earth.

V. The Disappearance

One morning, the scientists entered the room where the angel was being held and discovered that it was EMPTY. The doors were still locked, and the security systems had not recorded any intrusion or movement that would explain its absence. Subject 1510 had disappeared.

The company covered up the incident, stored the results of the investigation under strict security, and dispersed the documents among different archives. The case was classified, and, as the years passed, the fifth salvation eventually became yet another story FORGOTTEN DUE TO HUMANITY'S INGRATITUDE.

VI. Re-detection — The Corrupted Energy

Decades later, new generations of scientists — to whom the story of the fifth extinction had been passed down — took the place of those who had studied the mysterious entity. The development of much more advanced technology made it possible to detect something that until then had remained invisible.

A device designed to identify energy anomalies recorded energy at an indeterminate point on Earth. The energy partially matched that which, according to ancient records, had surrounded the angel.

But something was different. This energy was no longer golden. It seemed to harbor a DARKNESS THAT HAD NOT BEEN PRESENT BEFORE.

Two hypotheses were proposed:
A) The angel had returned.
B) The light that once saved humanity had been corrupted.

Hypothesis B gained traction. If true, the very being that had protected the world during the fifth mass extinction might have become a new threat.

The investigation took on a different purpose. They needed to find the subject. The case was reopened.

VII. Discovery of the Predecessor Archives

In the midst of the new investigation, the scientists discovered the existence of other archives. These were documents dating back even before the founding of the company that had studied the angel. They spoke of four entities that had appeared during previous extinctions and contained information that no modern scientific institution had managed to gather on its own.

Their records were written in strange terms and accompanied by drawings that were difficult to interpret, but they were relatively complete. There were dates, locations, descriptions, traces of their appearances, and, in some cases, testimonies from those who had witnessed their actions.

There was, however, a fifth archive. That one was slightly different, because it was incomplete. Most of its information remained locked away, and the more they tried to access it, the more evident it became that that protection had NOT BEEN CREATED BY HUMAN HANDS.

After countless attempts to breach it, the researchers found a single note hidden among the oldest documents:

"The archive will only be opened for its owner."

The information they were seeking — details about the Fifth's birth, his identity, his nature, and possibly the true purpose of his existence — was there. A revelation capable of altering our understanding of history remained locked behind a door that only one entity — whose whereabouts they did not even know — could open.

VIII. The Return of Subject 1510

The angel, too, had been searching for answers. For years he had wandered without fully understanding what he was, where he came from, or why fragments of an existence he could not remember seemed to haunt him. Until, somehow, he discovered that the company held information about him.

So he returned, this time of his own free will. The security systems detected the anomaly too late. The subject's presence spread throughout the facility before the researchers realized what was happening. Alarms began blaring in the hallways and containment protocols were activated, but NONE OF THEM MANAGED TO STOP HIM.

The scientists who tried to stand in his way lost their lives. And when silence once again took hold of the facility, the subject continued moving forward among the bodies and the emergency lights, driven by the desire to know who he was.

He went as far as the company's archives and began investigating on his own. He reviewed reports, files, photographs, experiment logs, and testimonies written decades ago. He found descriptions of his body, his blood, and his abilities. None of it provided him with the answer he was looking for.

Until he found the fifth file.

The very door that scientists had been trying to open for years stood before him — the one that had rejected every human code, device, and method used to breach its protection. He didn't need any of them because the file recognized him.

All the information the company had been pursuing for decades — all the data about his birth, his identity, and his true nature — had been safeguarded there, waiting not to be discovered, but for its owner to return and claim it.

Because the being who once descended from the heavens to save humanity had never truly disappeared. He had simply forgotten his name.

IX. Current Status & Containment Notes

After the breach, the facility was sealed. Recovery teams found CASUALTIES NUMBERING [REDACTED] and significant structural damage to the archive wing. The fifth file was EMPTY — its contents removed by the subject.

Energy monitoring stations detected a brief surge of golden radiance at the facility coordinates immediately following the breach, followed by a sustained reading of CORRUPTED ENERGY at an indeterminate global position. The subject's current location is UNKNOWN.

The investigation remains ACTIVE. All field agents are advised to approach with extreme caution. The being that saved humanity may no longer be what it was.
